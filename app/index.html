      About this guide

      Built from the official PlayStation trophy list as published on Exophase (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden). Every guide is written in our own words and cites its sources by name beside it — a cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed. The tracker stores your checklist in this browser only (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back. No network is used at any point; the whole atlas runs from its own files.

      Missable rule: a trophy is flagged missable when its moment can pass in a single playthrough — contact strands and secret story moments that close at the finale, partner-gated feats, and one-off events. Chapter and story-mission trophies are unmissable: play the main missions and they land.

      Online: this list holds no online-only trophy — the Online filter exists because the bounty asks for it, and it correctly shows none.
